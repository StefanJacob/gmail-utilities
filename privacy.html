Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy covers the personal utilities described on the Gmail Utilities home page. They are built and used by Stefan Jacob for his own Google account only. They are not offered to, or used by, anyone else.

What Google data is accessed

  • Gmail inventory uses read-only Gmail access (gmail.readonly) to read message headers (sender, date, subject, labels, read/unread status, mailing-list and unsubscribe headers) and the account's filter and forwarding settings. It does not send, change or delete email.
  • Drive backup (rclone) uses Google Drive access to copy files between the owner's computer and his own Google Drive.

How the data is used

Data is used only to provide these utilities to their owner: to analyse and organise his own inbox, and to back up his own files. This may include analysis with AI assistants the owner chooses to use for his own purposes.

Storage

Gmail header data is stored in a local database on the owner's own computer. Backed-up files are stored on the owner's computer and in his own Google Drive. Sign-in tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with, or transferred to any other person or organisation, and is not used for advertising.

Limited Use

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored data can be deleted by removing the utilities' project folders.
